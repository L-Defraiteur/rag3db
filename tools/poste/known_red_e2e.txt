# Known red cases of the e2e suites (19 Nov. 2026, form of c7): <suite>\t<case>\t<path of its open ticket>.
# The pinned pass plays the whole suite; a listed red case is said « rouge connu », a listed case that passes « à lever ».
# A line whose ticket is missing, closed or in closed/ refuses the pass. The lot that fixes a case removes its line.
